## 2. Full Description

**ilm AI — Your Complete AI-Powered Study Companion for Pakistan & India**

Struggling to keep up with board exams, past papers, and endless revision? ilm AI brings together an AI tutor, smart practice tools, and real exam-pattern preparation into one platform built specifically for Class 9 to 12 students in Pakistan and India — with support for school administrators, college administrators, teachers, and parents too.

Whether you're preparing for Matric, Intermediate (FSc/FA/ICS/ICom), board exams, or entrance tests, ilm AI helps you study smarter with tools that actually understand your syllabus.

**🎓 AI TUTOR — Ask, Learn, Understand**
Get instant, subject-aware explanations from your personal AI Tutor. Stuck on a tricky Physics numerical or a Biology concept? Ask in your own words and get clear, step-by-step help — anytime, in English or Urdu.

**📝 MCQs & PRACTICE TESTS**
Practice with structured multiple-choice questions organized by subject and chapter. Run full practice sessions, track your answers, and build exam confidence with a question bank designed around your actual curriculum.

**📄 PAST PAPERS**
Access organized past papers by subject, so you always know what to expect on exam day. Study real exam patterns instead of guessing what might come.

**🔮 GUESS PAPERS**
Get AI-assisted guess papers to focus your revision where it matters most before the exam — a smarter way to prioritize your limited study time.

**🃏 FLASHCARDS**
Create and review flashcard decks for quick memorization and spaced revision — perfect for vocabulary, formulas, dates, and definitions.

**📷 SCAN & SOLVE**
Snap a photo of a question or textbook page and let ilm AI's OCR-powered tools help you extract and work through it — no more retyping questions by hand.

**✍️ ESSAY WRITER & HUMANIZER**
Get structured help drafting essays for English and other subjects, plus tools to refine and naturalize AI-assisted writing.

**📚 STUDY LIBRARY & CLASS LIBRARY**
Browse organized textbooks, notes, and study resources by grade and subject — all in one themed, easy-to-read reader.

**🕌 QURAN SECTION**
A dedicated space for Quran learning and recitation practice, alongside your academic studies.

**🧒 KIDS ZONE**
A separate, playful learning zone for younger learners — with English, Urdu, Math, GK, Islamic studies, quizzes, memory games, stories, and listen-and-repeat activities designed to make early learning fun.

**🎥 LIVE CLASSES**
Join live class sessions for real-time teaching and interaction, run through the platform's live-class system.

**🏆 COMPETITIONS, LEADERBOARDS & ACHIEVEMENTS**
Compete with other students, climb the leaderboard, unlock achievements, and stay motivated with XP, streaks, and gamified progress tracking.

**🧭 CAREER GUIDANCE & UNIVERSITY HUB**
Explore career paths and get guidance on your next academic steps. Browse the University Hub to research university programs as you plan life after school or college.

**📊 PROGRESS, INSIGHTS & DIAGNOSTIC TESTS**
Take diagnostic tests to identify weak areas, then track your progress and performance insights over time so you always know where you stand.

**👨‍👩‍👧 PARENT DASHBOARD**
Parents can link to their child's account and view analytics on study activity and performance — staying involved without hovering.

**🏫 SCHOOL & COLLEGE ADMIN PANELS**
ilm AI isn't just for students. Schools and colleges get dedicated admin panels covering academics, admissions, attendance, exams, fees, payroll, communication, PTMs, people/staff management, and reporting — helping institutions run day-to-day operations digitally alongside student learning.

**👩‍🏫 TEACHER TOOLS**
Teachers can manage their classes and build tests directly within the platform.

**🌙 BILINGUAL & THEMED EXPERIENCE**
Study comfortably with support for English and Urdu content, plus a clean, themed reading experience across resources and notes.
